# Private research handoff

This repository transfers the owner's ADMET competition research to authorized collaborators.
No general redistribution or open-source license is granted by this handoff.
Competition data remains subject to its original terms and is included only in private release assets.
Pretrained ChemBERTa and TabICL models and third-party packages retain their respective licenses.
Do not publish the private data, conditioned estimators, or model artifacts without the owner's authorization.

This is an experimental prediction system, not a validated clinical decision tool.
